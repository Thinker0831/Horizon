This feature allows a session on a standby to wait until it has replayed the write-ahead log up to a specific log sequence number \(LSN\), ensuring that reads reflect recent writes made on the primary. The release was delayed by two months due to the need to address AI-reported vulnerabilities. The WAIT FOR LSN feature is similar to mechanisms previously implemented in systems like MediaWiki with MySQL.

### [How NVIDIA Dynamo Streamlines RL Rollout Infrastructure](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/reducing-rollout-bottlenecks-in-reinforcement-learning-with-nvidia-dynamo/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

rss · NVIDIA CUDA Technical Blog · Nov 19, 20:28

**「Background」** Scaling reinforcement learning \(RL\) training often runs into bottlenecks: redundant computation, slow rollout completion, inefficient weight updates, and worker failures can all waste GPU resources and delay progress. Traditional distributed RL setups struggle to balance uneven workloads, avoid recomputing shared context, and keep model weights fresh across many workers, especially as training complexity grows.

**「Solution」** The author describes how NVIDIA Dynamo addresses these RL infrastructure challenges by acting as a serving layer between the RL framework and inference workers. Dynamo introduces KV-aware routing, which intelligently directs requests to workers with cached prompts to minimize redundant computation, but also balances load to prevent bottlenecks from forming at any single worker. For model weight updates, Dynamo leverages ModelExpress, which enables workers to copy weights directly from peers \(using NIXL\) and supports delta weight transfer—sending only compressed changes rather than full models—to reduce network overhead and speed up updates. This mechanism also aids in recovery: when a worker fails, a replacement can quickly catch up by applying deltas to a base checkpoint, while healthy workers continue generating rollouts. Dynamo&\#x27;s architecture keeps serving and training logic separate, allowing RL frameworks to control sample acceptance and weight application, while Dynamo manages routing, worker health, and telemetry. The post cites customer anecdotes—such as CoreWeave achieving up to 15× faster weight updates and H Company reducing training-step time by up to 20%—to illustrate Dynamo&\#x27;s impact, though detailed benchmarks and tradeoff discussions are limited.

**「Takeaway」** NVIDIA Dynamo streamlines RL rollout infrastructure by reducing redundant computation, accelerating weight distribution, and improving recovery, enabling faster and more efficient distributed RL training iterations.
